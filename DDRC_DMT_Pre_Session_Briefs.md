# Full Course: MONDAY

## Foundations (7.5 hours)

Today covers the building blocks. You will work through the anatomy and physiology you need to understand injuries, the physics that govern gas behaviour underwater, the scope of the DMT role, and the principles of structured patient assessment.

### Today's Sessions

| Code | Topic | Duration | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| O1.1 | Physiology Review (musculoskeletal, nervous, cardiovascular, respiratory, ears) | 2.5h | SI |
| O1.2 | Diving Physics (gas laws, pressure/volume, partial pressures) | 2h | SI |
| O1.3 | DMT Role and Responsibilities (scope of practice, physician guidance) | 1h | SI |
| O1.4 | First Aid Principles (primary survey, ABCDE, secondary survey, SAMPLE) | 2h | SI |

### Have Ready

- DMAC 01 Aide-Memoire (downloaded PDF or printed copy)
- Notepad for sketching diagrams and noting terms
- Somewhere quiet with your camera on and mic working

### Key Terms You Will Encounter

| Term | Meaning | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ABCDE** | Primary survey sequence | **Boyle's law** | Pressure-volume relationship |
| **Dalton's law** | Partial pressures in gas mixtures | **Henry's law** | Gas solubility under pressure |
| **Tidal volume** | Volume of a normal breath (~500ml) | **Dermatome** | Skin area supplied by one nerve root |
| **SAMPLE** | History-taking mnemonic | **Tachycardia** | Heart rate above 100 bpm |

> **Think about this before the session:**
> - When you equalise your ears on descent, what do you think is physically happening inside your middle ear? What structures are involved?
> - If a casualty on deck is unresponsive, what is the very first thing you would do before touching them?

# Full Course: TUESDAY

## Diving Medicine and Medical Topics (6.5 hours)

Today moves into the conditions that are specific to diving and the broader medical emergencies a DMT may encounter. Gas toxicity, barotrauma, and a survey of medical conditions from head injuries to chest injuries to diabetic emergencies.

### Today's Sessions

| Code | Topic | Duration | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| O2.1 | Gas Toxicity (O2 toxicity, CO2 retention, CO poisoning, N2 narcosis, HPNS) | 2.5h | SI |
| O2.2 | Barotrauma and Ear Problems (squeeze types, ear/sinus barotrauma, Teed grading) | 2h | SI |
| O3.4 | Remaining Medical Topics (head injury, stroke, seizures, chest injury, diabetes, poisoning, bites) | 2h | SI |

### Have Ready

- DMAC 01 Aide-Memoire
- Your notes from Monday (particularly gas laws and the ABCDE framework)
- A calculator or the calculator on your phone (for partial pressure calculations)

### Key Terms You Will Encounter

| Term | Meaning | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ppO2** | Partial pressure of oxygen | **CNS toxicity** | Oxygen toxicity affecting the brain |
| **Barotrauma** | Tissue damage from pressure change | **Teed grading** | Ear barotrauma severity scale (0-5) |
| **Pneumothorax** | Air in the pleural space | **GCS** | Glasgow Coma Scale |
| **Hypoglycaemia** | Dangerously low blood sugar | **Narcosis** | Nitrogen narcosis at depth |

> **Think about this before the session:**
> - A diver at 40 metres is breathing air (21% O2). What is the partial pressure of oxygen they are breathing? Use Dalton's law from Monday.
> - Think about the last time you or someone you know had trouble equalising. What was happening, and what made it better or worse?

# Full Course: THURSDAY

## DCI and Pharmacology with the DMP (5 hours)

Thursday is taught by a Diving Medical Physician. This is the core diving medicine teaching: decompression illness in depth, followed by the pharmacology of the DMAC 15 drug kit. Refresher candidates will also be in the session. The DMP teaches at full depth for everyone.

### Thursday Sessions

| Code | Topic | Duration | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|
| O2.3 | Decompression Illness (pathophysiology, types, treatment, recompression tables) | 3h | DMP |
| O3.3 | Pharmacology and Infection Control (DMAC 15 drugs, routes, indications, Entonox) | 2h | DMP |

### Have Ready (Both Days)

- DMAC 01 Aide-Memoire (you will use this extensively on Thursday)
- All your notes from Monday and Tuesday
- The DMAC 15 drug list if you have it (covered in detail on Thursday)

### Key Terms (Wednesday)

| Term | Meaning | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hypovolaemic shock** | Shock from fluid/blood loss | **Anaphylaxis** | Severe systemic allergic reaction |
| **Rule of nines** | Burn surface area estimation | **Hypothermia** | Core temp below 35C |

### Key Terms (Thursday)

| Term | Meaning | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **DCI** | Decompression illness (umbrella term) | **AGE** | Arterial gas embolism |
| **Type I / Type II** | DCS classification by severity | **Recompression** | Treatment in a hyperbaric chamber |
| **DMAC 15** | Approved offshore medical kit list | **IM / IV / SC** | Injection routes |

> **Think about this before the session:**
> - A diver surfaces normally but 30 minutes later feels dizzy and has tingling in both hands. What could be happening, and what would you do first?

### Key Terms to Refresh

| Term | Meaning | Term | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ABCDE** | Primary survey (may have updates) | **SAMPLE/AMPLE** | Focused history-taking |

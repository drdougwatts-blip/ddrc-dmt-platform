## Short Answer Marking Guide

**Q26 (5 marks): DCI management**
Award 1 mark each for: DR ABCDE/systematic assessment; 100% O2; IV access and fluids; neurological examination and documentation; contact physician/prepare for recompression. Accept equivalent clinical actions.

**Q27 (3 marks): Recent developments**
Award 1 mark per relevant development identified and briefly described. Examples: updated resuscitation guidelines, new airway devices (i-gel), telemedicine systems, ECG monitoring requirements, COVID/infection control protocols, changes to DMAC 15 drug list, updated DCI treatment evidence. Must be specific, not generic.

**Q28 (2 marks): DCI evolution terms**
Relapsing (1 mark) and Progressive (1 mark).

**Q29 (3 marks): Burns calculation**
Anterior chest = 9% (1 mark). Both arms front and back = 18% (1 mark). Total = 27% (1 mark). Accept minor variations in working.

**Q30 (2 marks): Tension pneumothorax signs**
Award 1 mark per sign (any 2 of 3): absent breath sounds on the affected side, tracheal deviation away from the affected side, jugular venous distension. Also accept: increasing respiratory distress, falling SpO2, hypotension.

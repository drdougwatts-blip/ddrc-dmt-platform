## Welcome

Welcome back. Your DMT certificate is due for renewal, and this refresher course will bring you up to date with current guidance, refresh your practical skills, and confirm your continued competence.

The refresher is a 30-hour hybrid programme. It is shorter and more focused than the full course because it builds on your existing qualification and experience.

### How the Refresher Works

| | Phase 1: Online Review | Phase 2: In-Person Practical |
|---|---|---|
| **Duration** | 13 hours over 2 consecutive days (Wednesday and Thursday) | 18 hours over 2 consecutive days (9 hours each day) |
| **Format** | Live video sessions. Camera on. Interactive review of updates, current guidance, and refresher topics. | Full practical skills refresher at DDRC Plymouth. Same equipment and manikins as the full course. |

Your total commitment is 4 days: 2 online followed by 2 in person. The gap between online completion and the in-person phase should be no more than 8 weeks.

### Online Days at a Glance

| Day | Content | Hours | Instructor |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wednesday** | Resuscitation Update, Casualty Care Review, Communications and Records, New Techniques | 6.0 | SI |
| **Thursday** | DCI Update (DMP, shared), Pharmacology Update (DMP, shared), Diving Accidents Review | 7.0 | DMP / SI |

Wednesday is a full day of refresher-specific review led by a Senior DMT Instructor. Thursday morning and early afternoon are shared with the full course cohort for DCI and pharmacology updates, taught by a Diving Medical Physician. After the DMP finishes, you stay for a diving accidents review session using real incident case studies.

### In-Person Days at a Glance

Two intensive days at DDRC Plymouth (0800 to 1815). Day 1 covers BLS, airway management, vital signs, fractures, and a formative scenario. Day 2 covers IV access, injections, wound closure, neurological examination, your written exam, and your assessed practical scenario.

Day 2 runs slightly later than a standard day to accommodate both the assessment and the final practical skills.

### Eligibility

You should be within 3 months before or up to 8 weeks after the expiry date on your current DMT certificate. If your certificate expired more than 8 weeks ago, you will need to complete the full course instead. Please check your certificate dates and contact DDRC if you are unsure.

## Self-Assessment: Answers

| Q | Ans | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **B** | Joint pain and neurological symptoms after a dive are DCI until proven otherwise. The course will review current pathophysiology. |
| 2 | **B** | High-flow O2, horizontal, IV fluids, and urgent medical advice. Never attempt in-water recompression. |
| 3 | **C** | The DMAC 01 neurological section requires a structured assessment. You will practise this in person. |
| 4 | **B** | 30 compressions to 2 ventilations. Check your resuscitation update (RM1) for any recent guideline changes. |
| 5 | **B** | Nitrous oxide diffuses into gas spaces. In a DCI casualty with tissue bubbles, this can worsen the injury. The pharmacology session will cover this. |
| 6 | **B** | Teed Grade 3 = gross haemorrhage within the TM. Grade 4 = perforation. The grading scale will be reviewed in the course. |
| 7 | **C** | 94-100% is the normal range. Below 94% needs attention. Below 90% is a clinical emergency. |
| 8 | **B** | Outer mid-thigh (anterolateral). This gives reliable absorption and avoids inadvertent IV injection. |
| 9 | **B** | A thorough rapid neuro exam takes 5-10 minutes. Too fast and you miss things; too slow and you delay treatment. |
| 10 | **B** | ABCDE is a prioritisation framework: find and fix what will kill the patient first. |

**If you scored 7 or more:** Your knowledge base is solid. The course will update and refresh it.

**If you scored 4 to 6:** Fairly typical for someone who has not used their DMT skills daily. Review the DMAC 01 and your original course notes before Wednesday.

**If you scored below 4:** Worth spending some time with the DMAC 01 aide-memoire and a first aid manual before the course. The refresher covers a lot of ground in two online days.

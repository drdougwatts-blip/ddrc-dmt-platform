**SUPPLEMENTARY RESOURCE -- Outside Contact Hours**

*This workbook sits outside the 30-hour course contact time. It is not required for assessment and does not count towards D020 delivery hours. It is provided to support your learning.*

**How to Use This Workbook**

This workbook mirrors the structure of the DDRC DMT Refresher course. There is a section for every online module and every in-person session, in the order you will experience them.

As an experienced DMT, you already know much of this material. The workbook is designed to help you identify what has changed since you last qualified, consolidate areas where you may be rusty, and prepare effectively for the assessment.

**Online Phase Sections**

For each online module: learning objectives, prompted note-taking spaces (focused on changes and updates), and self-check questions. If you answer 'No' to any self-check question, raise it with the instructor before the in-person phase.

**In-Person Phase Sections**

For each practical session: learning objectives, a procedural checklist, and reflection prompts. The pace is faster than the full course. Use the checklists to confirm you have demonstrated each skill, and the reflections to note anything that felt less familiar than expected.

**SUPPLEMENTARY RESOURCE -- Outside Contact Hours**

*This workbook sits outside the 60-hour course contact time. It is not required for assessment and does not count towards D020 delivery hours. It is provided to support your learning.*

**How to Use This Workbook**

This workbook mirrors the structure of the DDRC DMT course. There is a section for every online module and every in-person session, in the order you will experience them.

It is not a textbook. It will not teach you the material on its own. Its purpose is to give you a structured place to capture what matters during each session, check your own understanding afterwards, and build a personal reference you can take back to work.

**Online Phase Sections**

For each online module you will find the learning objectives, prompted note-taking spaces for key concepts, and a set of self-check questions. Use the self-check questions after the session to test whether you have understood the core material. If you answer 'No' to any, go back to your notes or raise the topic with the instructor.

**In-Person Phase Sections**

For each practical session you will find the learning objectives, a procedural checklist of the key steps or skills covered, and reflection prompts. Use the checklist during or after the session to track what you have practised. The reflection prompts are for you to note what went well, what you found difficult, and what you want to revisit.

**Self-Check Confidence Column**

The self-check tables have a 'Confident?' column. Be honest with yourself. A 'No' is not a failure. It is a signal to revisit the topic before the assessment day.
